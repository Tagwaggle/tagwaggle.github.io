About Me

Who I Am

I’m Joseph (Joey) Guynes — educator, mentor, operations leader, and tech enthusiast with a knack for building systems that make life easier for the people around me. Whether I’m guiding students through complex concepts, optimizing workflows, or spinning up a new homelab service, I bring clarity, structure, and a little personality to everything I do.

I’ve spent over a decade leading teams in retail and pharmacy operations before transitioning into higher education, where I’ve supported hundreds of students across Marketing, UX Design, Communications, and Power Skills courses. My approach blends empathy, problem‑solving, and technical fluency — because people deserve support that’s both human and effective.

What Drives Me

  • Helping people grow into their potential
  • Building systems that make work smoother and smarter
  • Turning ambiguity into clarity
  • Creating learning experiences that actually feel good
  • Exploring new tech, tools, and ways to solve problems
